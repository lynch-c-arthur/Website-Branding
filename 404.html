Arthur Lynch

Arthur Lynch is a former University of Georgia football captain and All-SEC tight end, 2014 NFL Draft pick (155th overall, Miami Dolphins), OCS-commissioned US Army infantry officer, Jefferies investment banking summer associate (TMT), and 2nd-year MBA candidate at Cornell University's Johnson Graduate School of Management (Class of 2026). He leads investment banking recruiting for the Old Ezra Finance Club, the oldest student-led club on campus.

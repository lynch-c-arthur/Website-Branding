Career Milestones

Professional Timeline

2009 – 2013

University of Georgia

Tight End & Offensive Team Captain — Bulldogs Football (SEC)

Four-year letterman, All-SEC recognition, and elected offensive team captain during senior season. Key contributor on the 2011 and 2012 squads that won the SEC East and competed in back-to-back SEC Championship Games. Accepted invitation to the Senior Bowl.

January 2014

Senior Bowl & NFL Combine

NFL Draft Preparation

Competed at the Senior Bowl in Mobile, Alabama, and the NFL Scouting Combine in Indianapolis. Showcased athletic ability and football intelligence for NFL scouts, solidifying draft stock as a top tight end prospect.

May 2014

2014 NFL Draft — Miami Dolphins

5th Round, 155th Overall Pick

Selected by the Miami Dolphins in the 5th round of the 2014 NFL Draft, becoming the 155th overall pick. Joined one of the most competitive rosters in the AFC East as a professional tight end.

2014 – 2017

NFL Career — Four Teams

Miami Dolphins, New York Jets, Denver Broncos, Atlanta Falcons

Competed at the highest level of professional football across four NFL franchises. Career was cut short by multiple back injuries and surgeries, leading to retirement in 2017.

2019

US Army Officer Candidate School

Commissioned as Infantry Officer

Overcame initial medical rejections — with advocacy from Senator Elizabeth Warren and Congressman Bill Keating — to gain admission to OCS. Completed one of the Army's most demanding commissioning programs and earned commission as an infantry officer.

2019 – 2022

US Army — Infantry Officer & Executive Officer

Executive Officer (XO) — Logistics & Operations

Served as Executive Officer (XO), managing logistics and operations in high-pressure environments. Led soldiers with the same grit and discipline demonstrated on the gridiron. Developed expertise in organizational leadership, risk management, and team building.

2021

NYC Marathon — Haymakers for Hope

Charity Endurance Event

Completed the 2021 TCS New York City Marathon running on behalf of Haymakers for Hope, raising funds for cancer research.

2024

Cornell University — Johnson MBA

MBA, Class of 2026 — Investment Banking

Enrolled at Cornell Johnson to pursue an MBA specializing in investment banking. Elected by peers to lead IB recruiting for the Old Ezra Finance Club, mentoring 100+ first-year students preparing for Wall Street.

Summer 2025

Jefferies — IB Summer Associate

Technology, Media & Telecommunications (TMT)

Investment banking summer associate at Jefferies in the TMT group, working across Private Capital Advisory and Leveraged Finance. Successfully completed the program and received a full-time return offer.

May 2026

Cornell Johnson MBA — Graduated

Master of Business Administration

Graduated from the Samuel Curtis Johnson Graduate School of Management at Cornell University with an MBA in investment banking.

Arthur Lynch

Arthur Lynch is a former University of Georgia football captain and All-SEC tight end, 2014 NFL Draft pick (155th overall, Miami Dolphins), OCS-commissioned US Army infantry officer, Jefferies investment banking summer associate (TMT), and Cornell MBA from the Johnson Graduate School of Management (Class of 2026). At Johnson, he led investment banking recruiting for the Old Ezra Finance Club, the oldest student-led club on campus.

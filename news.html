Arthur Lynch

Arthur Lynch is a former University of Georgia football captain, 2014 NFL Draft pick (Miami Dolphins), OCS-commissioned US Army infantry officer, finance professional, and current MBA candidate at Cornell University's Johnson Graduate School of Management. His career reflects a commitment to leadership, discipline, and excellence across every arena.
